Method

Why a blend, and why it adds spread

Averaging three models smooths away the strongest winds. A plain average forecasts only about 70% of the gale area that verifies. Adding a quarter of the ensemble spread, capped at 5 kt, puts that area back without inflating the calm regions.

The recipe was tuned on 145 hindcast cases and then checked on 200 cases it had never seen, scored against the GFS and ECMWF analyses and against buoy and ship reports. It was tested on 30–67°N in the two OPC basins, and the map marks that area.
